Voltscope Review

Extract, validate and correct UK energy bills

What it is

Voltscope reads photos or PDFs of UK energy bills and turns them into structured, checked data. Upload a bill and it pulls out every field — supplier, meter points, tariffs, reads and charges — validates the figures, and flags anything that looks wrong.

Built with

Python, FastAPI and Pydantic on the back end, SQLite for storage, and the Anthropic Claude API for reading the bills, with a dependency-free vanilla-JavaScript interface. All validation and analytics are deterministic — no model calls, no build step — and the whole thing is test-covered and deploy-ready.

Why it helps

It catches money leaks: duplicate charges, estimated reads that tend to overcharge, and totals that don’t reconcile. It warns when a fixed contract is nearing its end so you can switch before out-of-contract rates hit, and compares spend and unit rates across every supplier in your portfolio.
